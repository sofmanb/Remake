About us

London's data centre specialists

VIRTUS designs, builds and operates data centres for the world's leading cloud, enterprise and AI companies.

Our story

VIRTUS was founded to bring flexible, efficient data centre capacity to London. Since then it has grown into one of the capital's largest providers, operating eleven facilities across four campuses.

In 2017 VIRTUS became a wholly owned part of ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC), one of the world's fastest-growing data centre operators, giving customers access to an international platform with a London focus.
